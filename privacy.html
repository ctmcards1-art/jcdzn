Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Who we are. J C Designs (jcdzn.com) is a web design studio in Maine run by Colby Moholland and Jack Caricofe.

What we collect. When you send our contact form, we receive your name, email address, the type of project you picked and your message. The form is delivered to our email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).

Cookies and ad tracking. This site uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. It uses cookies to record visits and form submissions so we can measure our Facebook and Instagram ads and show ads to people who have visited this site. Meta may collect information such as your IP address, browser and pages viewed. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook ad settings at facebook.com/adpreferences, opt out at optout.aboutads.info, or block cookies in your browser.

How we use it. We use your contact details only to reply to you and work on your project. We don't sell your information.

Your choices. To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, email us and we'll take care of it.
